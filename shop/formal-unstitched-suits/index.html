Ordering, delivery and availability

How can I order? Use the website Cash on Delivery checkout or a product’s official WhatsApp button. Website COD orders are confirmed by phone before dispatch.

How current are price and availability? Catalogue updates run approximately every 12 hours. Final availability, price and delivery charges are confirmed before dispatch.

Can I cancel? Before the confirmation call, contact Al Huma Collection on official WhatsApp and share the relevant order details.
