Al Huma YouTube Playlist Manager

Status: This is an internal application being configured and tested. Automated playlist management is not yet operational.

Purpose

This application is intended for authorized Al Huma Collection personnel to organize videos and Shorts published on the Al Huma Collection YouTube channel into relevant playlists. Planned organization includes monthly new-arrival playlists and evergreen collections for product categories and shopping guidance.

How it works

After a channel administrator explicitly authorizes access through Google's OAuth consent process, the application is intended to use the YouTube Data API v3 to read channel video and playlist information, create playlists when required, and add relevant published videos to playlists while avoiding duplicate entries.

Access and availability

The application is intended for Al Huma Collection's own channel administration, not as a public consumer service. It is separate from the Al Huma Collection online store and does not process customer orders or payments.

Privacy and permissions

Google authorization is optional and can be revoked. The requested YouTube management scope is broader than the playlist operations the application intends to perform. Please review the application privacy policy before authorizing access.

Contact

For questions about this application or its use of YouTube account information, email alhumacollection@gmail.com.
