YouTube Playlist Manager Privacy Policy

Current status: The application is under configuration. The automated YouTube playlist workflow described below is planned and has not yet been activated.

Who operates this application

Al Huma Collection, Sialkot, Pakistan, operates the Al Huma YouTube Playlist Manager for internal administration of its own YouTube channel. Privacy inquiries: alhumacollection@gmail.com.

Information accessed and purpose

With explicit Google OAuth authorization, the planned application will access YouTube channel identity, published video identifiers and metadata (such as titles, descriptions and publication dates), and playlist identifiers, titles and membership. These details will be used to classify published videos, create appropriate playlists and insert videos without duplication.

The Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube grants broad YouTube account management access. The planned application will limit its own operations to channel verification and playlist organization; it will not intentionally delete videos, upload videos, change video visibility or manage comments.

Authorization and credentials

Authorization will be performed through Google's OAuth flow by an authorized channel administrator. OAuth client secrets and access/refresh tokens must be stored in private, access-restricted systems, not published in the website or its public repository. No OAuth tokens are collected by these informational webpages.

Storage and retention

The planned automation may retain the minimum channel, video and playlist identifiers, classification decisions, processing status and operational logs necessary to prevent duplicates and troubleshoot errors. Access will be restricted to authorized administrators. Authorization tokens will be retained only while required for the integration and will be deleted when the integration is permanently disconnected. Operational records will be reviewed and removed when no longer necessary for administration or audit.

Sharing and advertising

The application is not intended to sell YouTube account information or use it for advertising profiling. Information may be processed by Google/YouTube as the API provider and by the private infrastructure selected to run the automation, solely to operate the authorized workflow. Any material change to these practices will require this policy to be updated before deployment.

Control, revocation and deletion

An authorized user may revoke this application's Google access at Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops new authorized API operations but does not automatically undo playlists previously created on YouTube. To request removal of retained integration data or to disconnect the automation, email alhumacollection@gmail.com. Requests will be handled by the channel administrator, subject to applicable operational and legal obligations.

Google and YouTube policies

Use of YouTube features is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. This application's privacy policy applies specifically to the intended Al Huma Collection playlist-management integration.

Updates

This notice is dated 12 October 2026. It will be revised if the implementation or its data practices materially change.
